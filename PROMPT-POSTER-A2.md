Use my thesis book as the primary source of truth, and the IEEE paper as a

Discussion
Summarize the key findings, their interpretation, and their practical

Conclusion
State the main conclusions, the contributions, and the future work.

Footer

## Key numbers that must appear and must be exact

Dataset: 251 monthly observations, July 2005 to May 2026, 12 domestic and
global macroeconomic indicators, 17 engineered features.

Experiment: 384 preprocessing combinations, six models, five phases.

Test performance at optimal configuration:
Random Forest RMSE 0.1434, MAPE 1.95, R2 0.8911
BiLSTM RMSE 0.1463, MAPE 2.01, R2 0.8867
LSTM RMSE 0.1620, MAPE 2.30, R2 0.8610
XGBoost RMSE 0.2207, MAPE 3.18, R2 0.7420
ARIMA RMSE 0.8974, negative R2
VAR RMSE 2.7089, strongly negative R2

Regime split over 36 test months, 9 rate-change and 27 rate-hold:
Random Forest RMSE 0.214 change and 0.110 hold, directional accuracy 33 percent
BiLSTM RMSE 0.162 change and 0.141 hold, directional accuracy 67 percent
LSTM RMSE 0.179 change and 0.156 hold, directional accuracy 67 percent
XGBoost RMSE 0.241 change and 0.213 hold, directional accuracy 67 percent

Explainability: mean absolute SHAP of the one-month BI-Rate lag is 0.9611.
Persistence share reaches 75.5 percent on Random Forest but only 6.7 percent on
LSTM. After removing persistence, the leading macroeconomic drivers are credit
growth, the real interest rate, M2, and the policy stance direction.

Ablation: removing the one-month lag raises Random Forest RMSE from 0.1434 to
0.4087, while leaving both recurrent models almost unchanged. Removing all
macroeconomic indicators improves aggregate RMSE but lowers directional
accuracy on rate-change months for every model.

Expert evaluation: one Bank Indonesia monetary economist, 96 assessments,
overall 3.99 of 5. Per model, LSTM 4.21, BiLSTM 4.00, Random Forest 3.92,
XGBoost 3.83. Hallucination absence and SHAP consistency both scored full marks.

State the honest finding plainly: the three leading models are practically
equivalent, their differences fall within training variance, and the aggregate
accuracy is largely inherited from the persistence of the series. The
contribution lies in the transparency layer.

## Writing style

Write everything in English. The language must be academic, professional,
clear, objective, human, concise, complete, coherent, direct, and easy to
understand.

Avoid marketing language, promotional wording, overly sophisticated vocabulary,
flowery phrasing, model-sounding expressions, repetition, redundancy, and
unnecessary adjectives. Do not write to sound impressive. Write to be
understood.

Every sentence should follow a proper grammatical structure, preferably
subject, verb, and object, optionally with a complement. Each sentence should
express one main idea.

For numbered lists, do not place a period after the number, and do not use
decorative bullets. When an item needs explanation, place the explanation on
the line below the item.

Do not use coded labels such as T1, T2, B1, B2, RM1, or RM2. Write the thing
out instead.

Keep technical terms that are more common in English in English.

## Text length

Keep every section concise. For labels inside figures, workflow components,
functions, modules, or classes, use at most five words. Short labels are
preferred as long as clarity survives.

## Layout quality

Ensure there is no overlapping object, no intersecting component, no cropped
figure, no overflowing text, and no hidden element. Keep alignment consistent,
margins consistent, spacing equal, and visual balance proper. Maintain
sufficient white space throughout.

## Color

Use a modern academic palette taken from the defense slides. Keep contrast
high. Keep the palette small. Maintain accessibility.

## Icons

Use simple professional icons only where they help. Do not use emoji. Do not
use decorative symbols. Do not use separator strings such as repeated equals
signs or dashes. Do not add graphical ornaments.

## Final deliverable

Produce a publication-quality academic poster suitable for an undergraduate
thesis defense and a research exhibition. The poster should represent the
thesis faithfully while remaining visually appealing, highly readable, and
ready for A2 printing. A reader should grasp the work within a few minutes.

Before generating the layout, present your structured plan first, covering the
column structure, what goes in each section, and which figure you will place
where. Wait for my approval, then produce the poster.
